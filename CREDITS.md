[Smashicons](https://www.flaticon.com/authors/smashicons) from Flaticon.

- `res/icon/hdd-color-outline.png`, `res/icon/hdd-blue.png`: the same icon in its color-with-black-outline and

- `res/IroncladDefrag.ico`: built from `res/icon/hdd-color.png` by `tools/make-icon.ps1` (16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64 and
  256 px).
